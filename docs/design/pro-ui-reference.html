Type and numbers

  • The platform font (SF Pro on iOS) on Flutter's Material 3 type scale: screen titles 26–31 bold, body 15, labels 12–13.
  • Tabular figures wherever digits line up (FontFeature.tabularFigures()).
  • Money in rows and detail keeps two decimals; summary tiles may round to whole dollars (OC-12).
  • Every number's screen-reader label names the quantity, and numbers and reasons wrap at large text sizes (docs/conventions.md §9).

Components, all stock Material 3

  • NavigationBar with five destinations; Record is a filled destination, not a floating button.
  • FilterChip and ChoiceChip for tickers, dates and counts; SegmentedButton for put/call.
  • Card, FilledButton, FilledButton.tonal, OutlinedButton, TextButton, SwitchListTile, outlined TextField.
  • The snapshot sheet is a modal bottom sheet; Record a trade and the paywall are full-screen routes.

Stage 1 · one tap from Today

Record a trade

For a trade already placed at the broker. Six required inputs, and a repeat ticker needs about five taps plus typing strike and credit. The owner's timing check sets the bar: a median under 20 seconds.

  1. 1Recently used tickers as one-tap chips, including tickers from closed cycles. Stage 1
  2. 2Put or call. With Call selected, the call attaches to a share-holding cycle that has no open call, or one line says why it can't be recorded here (see variants). D-P12
  3. 3The next four Fridays as chips, plus Other date. DTE is derived. The existing non-Friday warning applies to Other dates. Stage 1
  4. 4Per share by default. The Total per contract preference relabels this field and divides by 100. A put's credit bound is its strike, so no stock price is needed. A call with no stock price skips the check. FI-20 · FI-21
  5. 5Contracts stepper, starting at 1. Stage 1
  6. 6On by default and set per leg. brief-ledger §3.2
  7. 7Optional fields collapsed. A blank fee is stored as not recorded, never as $0. Stage 1 · brief-ledger §3.1
  8. 8Yield and capital committed shown before saving: 0.34 ÷ 19 × 365 ÷ 18 = 36%; 19 × 100 × 2 = $3,800. Stage 1
  9. 9Saves exactly what Track this position saves: an underlying, a cycle, the first leg and its reminders. At 18 DTE the 21-day reminder has already passed, so two are scheduled. Opening a fourth cycle on the free plan shows the paywall instead (Wave 2). Stage 1 · D-P2

Stage 2 · opened from T's row on Today

Update snapshot, with a preview

The preview on this page is live. Edit the mark, stock price, delta or IV and it re-runs the gate order: profit target, assignment, roll band, tail, then leave. In the app the preview comes from classify() itself, never from widget logic.

  3. 3Stock price was typed. IV is carried forward from the Sep 17 snapshot and stays marked until edited. Stage 2 · OC-6
  4. 4Delta goes in exactly as shown, sign included, with a live magnitude readout and a per-snapshot convention. Gates read the magnitude only, so flipping the convention never changes the bucket. conventions §2
  5. 5The bucket these numbers would produce, with its reason, captured %, roll band and its source, and extrinsic remaining. It uses the leg's pinned rule version (Standard v1 here). Stage 2

For the planner

Open choices

Choices this reference made that no brief decides. The planner ratifies or changes each one and logs it in the wave's plan; until then none of them is binding.

  1. OC-1Bucket counts follow the list's sort order: Assign, Roll, Close, Leave, then No data.
  2. OC-2"Premium collected this month" means credits received minus buybacks paid, by trade date, before fees. September in the sample: $719 − $158 = $561.
  3. OC-3Current capital committed includes legs past expiration until they're recorded, and says so.
  4. OC-4The inline Update button appears only on rows that need a reading; every other row opens the detail sheet.
  5. OC-5The sort control reads "By bucket". Today's menu says "Bucket severity", and severity is language the bucket names avoid.
  6. OC-6A carried-forward value stays marked until the user edits it. Today's sheet shows the helper line whatever happens.
  7. OC-7The share card's return on capital is the sum of net results over the sum of peak capital, for cycles whose end date falls in the month, before fees.
  8. OC-8Net delta leaves out legs past expiration as well as positions with no reading, and lists both.
  9. OC-9The paywall lists launch features only, derives the per-month figure from the store price, and makes no savings claim.
  10. OC-10In the light theme, bucket pills carry a hairline edge for shape; the dark theme doesn't need one.
  11. OC-11Assigned and called-away legs count as 100% premium capture, as the Journal counts them today. Four of the six September legs here are 100%, which pulls the median up to 82%.
  12. OC-12Summary tiles on Today and Portfolio round money to whole dollars; rows and the detail sheet keep two decimals (brief-ledger §8).
